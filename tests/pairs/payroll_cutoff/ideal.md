# Payroll Submission Deadlines (2026 Schedule)

## Who this is for

Team leads who submit timesheet totals for hourly staff. Salaried staff are not
covered by this page.

## Monthly cut-off

Totals must be submitted by 12:00 on the 18th of the month. The 18th is a hard
cut-off, not a target. Submissions that arrive after it are held for the
following month's run.

If the 18th falls on a weekend or a public holiday, the cut-off moves to the
last working day before it.

## Superseded 2025 deadlines

The 2025 schedule set the cut-off at 15:00 on the 20th of the month, and moved
it to the following Monday when the 20th fell on a weekend. Both are superseded
by the 2026 times above and are recorded here only so a reader who remembers
them can see they have changed.

## What to submit

Submit one file per team. The file must list every hourly worker with hours and
the cost centre the hours are charged to. Blank rows are rejected by the upload
page.

## Corrections

A correction submitted before the cut-off replaces the earlier file entirely.
After the cut-off, corrections go to the payroll inbox as a written request and
are applied to the following month.

## Paper timesheets

Teams without terminal access may send paper timesheets to the payroll office by
internal post, to arrive no later than the cut-off.

## Escalation

If the upload page is unavailable in the hour before the cut-off, email the
payroll team and keep the file. Do not send totals in the body of an email.
