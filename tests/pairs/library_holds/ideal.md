# Hold Shelf Management

## Placing an item on the shelf

When a requested item arrives, print a hold slip and fold it into the item so the
name is visible from the spine. Shelve items alphabetically by the borrower's
surname.

You can reserve an item that is on loan or held at another branch. We will email
you when it is ready to collect.

## How long an item waits

An item waits on the hold shelf for 7 days. Day one is the day the borrower is
notified, not the day the item arrived.

Bring your library card. Items are on the hold shelf under your surname. You have
7 days from the day we email you.

## Clearing the shelf

Sweep the shelf every morning before opening. Items past 7 days go back into
circulation, or on to the next borrower in the queue if there is one.

## Borrowers who cannot collect in time

A borrower who asks before the 7 days are up can have the item held for a further
7 days. This is granted once per request.

If you cannot get in within the week, ask us before the 7 days are up and we will
hold the item for another 7 days. We can do this once per reservation.

## Items nobody collects twice

An item that has been on the hold shelf twice without being collected goes back
to the shelves and the request is cancelled.

An item you do not collect goes back into circulation or on to the next person
waiting. If this happens twice with the same reservation, the reservation is
cancelled and you will need to reserve it again.
