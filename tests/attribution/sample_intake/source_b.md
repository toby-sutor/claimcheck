# Sample Intake - Laboratory Technician

## Booking the collection

Book the collection slot in the transport app the day before. Print the two
consignment labels and put one on the box and one in your folder.

## On arrival at the clinic

Ask the reception desk for the sealed transport box. Check the tamper seal
number against the collection note. Get the note signed and keep the top copy.

## Opening the box

Break the tamper seal at the bench and record the seal number in LIMS. Read the
temperature strip inside the lid and enter the reading against the consignment.

## Accessioning

Give each tube an accession number from the LIMS worklist. Reject any tube whose
label does not match the collection note, and raise a discrepancy in LIMS.
