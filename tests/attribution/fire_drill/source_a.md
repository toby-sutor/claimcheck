# Fire Drill - Ground Floor Wardens

## Before the drill

Collect the ground floor warden tabard and the sweep card from the box by the
reception lift. Check that the torch on the sweep card holder works.

## Sweeping the floor

Walk the ground floor in the order printed on the sweep card. Open every meeting
room door and call out. Do not enter the plant room; it is swept by engineering.

## At the assembly point

Report the sweep result to the fire officer at the assembly point by the north
gate. Say the floor, your name, and whether the sweep was complete.

## After the all-clear

Return the tabard and sweep card to the box by the reception lift and sign the
drill sheet.
