# Fire Drill Routine

## Before the drill

Collect the ground floor warden tabard and the sweep card from the box by the
reception lift. Check that the torch on the sweep card holder works.

## Sweeping the floor

Walk the ground floor in the order printed on the sweep card. Open every meeting
room door and call out. Do not enter the plant room; it is swept by engineering.

## Recording a room that could not be checked

Write the room number on the back of the sweep card if a door is locked or a
room is occupied by a contractor. The fire officer follows those up afterwards.

## If the drill is a real alarm

Do not go back in for the tabard or the sweep card. Report to the fire officer
and say that the sweep was not completed.
