# Ticket Triage - Service Desk

## Picking up a ticket

Take the oldest unassigned ticket in the queue rather than the one you like the
look of. Assign it to yourself before you start reading it.

## Setting the priority

A ticket that stops someone working is P2. A ticket that stops a whole team
working is P1. Everything else is P3 and is answered within 2 working days.

## Merging duplicates

Two tickets about one outage become one ticket. Link the second to the first and
close the second with a comment naming the ticket it was merged into.

## Escalating

Escalate a P1 to the on-call engineer immediately and a P2 after 4 working
hours. Escalation is a phone call, not a comment on the ticket.
