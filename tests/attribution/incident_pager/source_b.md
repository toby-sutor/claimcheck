# Incident Pager - Duty Manager

## Acknowledging a page

Acknowledge the page in the pager app within 5 minutes. An unacknowledged page
escalates to the secondary on-call after 5 minutes and to the duty manager
after 10.

## First checks

Open the service dashboard and read the alert that fired. Check whether the
alert belongs to a service your team owns. If it does not, reassign the
incident to the owning team and write down why you reassigned it.

## Approving customer communications

Draft the customer notice together with the communications lead and approve it
before anyone publishes it. No notice about a live incident leaves the company
without your approval.

## Notifying the executive sponsor

Call the executive sponsor for any incident still open after 60 minutes. Send a
written summary within 24 hours of the incident closing.
