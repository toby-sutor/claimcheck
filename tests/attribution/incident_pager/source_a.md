# Incident Pager - On-Call Engineer

## Acknowledging a page

Acknowledge the page in the pager app within 5 minutes. An unacknowledged page
escalates to the secondary on-call after 5 minutes and to the duty manager
after 10.

## First checks

Open the service dashboard and read the alert that fired. Check whether the
alert belongs to a service your team owns. If it does not, reassign the
incident to the owning team and write down why you reassigned it.

## Restarting a service

You may restart any service in the production estate from the deploy console.
Record every restart in the incident timeline with the time and the reason.
Restarts during a change freeze need the release manager's sign-off first.

## Handing over at end of shift

Write a handover note in the incident channel before you sign off. List every
open incident, what you have already tried, and who you have told.
