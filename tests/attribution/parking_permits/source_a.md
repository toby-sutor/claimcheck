# Parking Permits

## Where the permit goes

Display the permit face up on the dashboard so the whole of the printed date is
readable from outside the car.

## Bays you may use

Any bay marked with a white line is available to permit holders. Bays marked in
yellow are reserved and are enforced between 07:00 and 19:00 on weekdays.

## Renewing a staff permit

Apply through the intranet form in the last week of the month. A staff permit
runs from the 1st and is not backdated. Payroll deducts the fee over 12 months.

## Losing a staff permit

Report a lost permit to the facilities team the same day. A replacement costs 15
pounds and the old permit number is cancelled overnight.
