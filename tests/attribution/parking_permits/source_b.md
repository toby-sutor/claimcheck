# Parking Permits - Visitor Information

## Where the permit goes

Display the permit face up on the dashboard so the whole of the printed date is
readable from outside the car.

## Bays you may use

Any bay marked with a white line is available to permit holders. Bays marked in
yellow are reserved and are enforced between 07:00 and 19:00 on weekdays.

## Getting a visitor permit

Your host collects the permit from reception on the morning of your visit. A
visitor permit is valid for one day and shows the date of issue.

## Leaving the site

Return the permit to reception on your way out. A permit that is not returned is
charged to the host's department at 15 pounds.
