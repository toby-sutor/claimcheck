# Descaling the Office Kettle

## How often

Descale the kettle on the first Monday of the month. Put a note on the
whiteboard so the next person knows it has been done.

## What to use

Use the descaler sachets in the cupboard above the sink. One sachet does one
kettle. Do not use vinegar; it leaves a taste for days.

## Doing it

Fill to the 1 litre mark, add the sachet, and leave it for 20 minutes without
switching the kettle on. Empty it, then boil and empty twice with clean water.

## If the element is still furred

Leave the kettle unplugged with a note on it and email facilities. A furred
element trips the socket and takes the whole bank of sockets down with it.
