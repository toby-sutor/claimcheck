# Kettle Maintenance

## How often

Descale the kettle on the first Monday of the month. Put a note on the
whiteboard so the next person knows it has been done.

## What to use

Use the descaler sachets in the cupboard above the sink. One sachet does one
kettle. Do not use vinegar; it leaves a taste for days.

## Checking the lid seal

Press the lid closed and look for steam escaping at the hinge. A lid that does
not seal makes the kettle cut out before it boils.

## Replacing the filter

The mesh filter behind the spout lifts out. Rinse it under the tap every month
and replace it once a year; spares are in the same cupboard as the sachets.
